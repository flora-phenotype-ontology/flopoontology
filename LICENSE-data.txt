FLOPO Trait Atlas: licence of the data

The annotation data of the FLOPO Trait Atlas (http://phenomebrowser.net/flopo/) are licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This covers the trait records, their annotation classes and taxon axioms, the merged ranges, the
taxon matching, and the tables made from them. It does not cover:

- the Flora Phenotype Ontology, which is CC0 1.0 (LICENSE-ontology.txt);
- the code, which is BSD (LICENSE);
- the sentences quoted from the floras, which are included so that annotations can be checked and
  whose rights remain with their sources;
- the other ontologies and the taxon names (Plant Ontology, PATO, IPNI, World Checklist of
  Vascular Plants, World Flora Online), which keep their own terms.

Each data release carries this statement with the credit line to use (downloads/LICENSE.txt) and
a machine-readable description (void.ttl).
