## Body

PATO:0001732 is labelled for "any body part" but defined as a plant's disposition to shed no body part. Botanical "persistent" (for example a persistent calyx) means that one organ stays attached beyond the stage at which such organs usually detach. That quality inheres in the organ, whereas the shedability qualities (PATO:0001729) inhere in the organism that sheds. We propose a new organ-level "persistent" quality, possibly outside shedability, instead of relabelling PATO:0001732, whose referent would change. Optional additions to the same issue: fringed (PATO:0040008) has a textile-oriented definition, and falciform (PATO:0002215) carries "hooked", "uncinate" and "unciform" as EXACT synonyms although a small attachment hook need not be sickle-shaped (rows 44, 146).
